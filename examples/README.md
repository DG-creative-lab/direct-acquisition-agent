# Platform examples

Use these entry points to understand the current product and prepare a demo.
The examples use authored material and fictional contacts; they do not establish
customer demand, live inventory or campaign effectiveness.

| Example | What it shows | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Contextual YouTube walkthrough](../docs/youtube-preparation.md) and [ad samples](youtube-ad-samples/README.md) | An authored placement package, review/manual export and inspectable creative/media | Fictional placement catalogue; prerecorded media; no live targeting or qualified-action measurement |
| [Fielddesk preparation](fielddesk-profile/README.md) | A fictional visit-planning product, explicit profile/taxonomy, grounded fixture copy and an exact unapproved proposal | Recorded synthetic date; no provider calls, approvals, dispatch or measured outcomes |
| [Recorded outcome fixture](../docs/external-outcomes.md#run-the-recorded-fixture) | Explicit assignment/report identity, missingness and advisory statistical results | Authored recorded data, not an authenticated CRM feed |
| [Your own reviewed company context](../docs/intake-email-preparation.md) | Saved source assertions, exact review and fictional email export | Operator-reviewed assertions and synthetic contacts; no sending or independently verified source truth |

Fielddesk is a fictional example, not a real customer or service. Its authored
specification has a declared evidence window. The recorded example explains
that window and its simulated clock; a new live task must use current permitted
evidence and the actual authority checks.

For pitching, use the [design-partner brief](../docs/product-discovery.md) and
make the example's status visible. Choose one relevant job instead of presenting
all channels as a finished product.

Historical example directories retain original identities where recovery and
validation need them. The [legacy compatibility reference](../docs/legacy-product-demo.md)
explains that material; it is not the current platform default narrative.
